# King of the Jungle: Story

## Badger

**Intro**

Badger has never backed down from anything, and has never once asked why.

So when the drums started in his chest, the ones only he could hear, he did not ask. He sharpened his claws, strapped on his father's old armor, and walked toward the tournament.

He tells everyone he wants the crown. He does not know who taught him to want it.

**Clue after fight 3**

The Wolf he just defeated stares at Badger's armor. "That crest on your shoulder. A crowned claw. I have seen it before."

Badger has worn that armor his whole life. He never once looked at the crest.

**Clue after fight 6**

Scratched inside the old armor's collar, worn almost smooth, are words in his father's hand: "Eleven went up. If I do not come back down, do not follow."

**Clue after fight 9**

Every night now, the same dream: a tower of black stone, taller than the trees, standing where no tower should be. Badger wakes up digging at the dirt, trying to get under it.

**Ending**

Badger stands at the top of the Tower with the crown in his claws, and feels nothing.

The drums have not stopped. They have only gotten louder, and now they are coming from somewhere higher still.

## Wolf

**Intro**

Wolf left the pack the night the moon turned red. He could not explain it to them. He could barely explain it to himself.

His mother left on a night just like this one, when he was a pup. The pack says she abandoned them. Wolf has never believed it.

Something is calling him toward the crown, and it sounds like her howl.

**Clue after fight 3**

The beaten Hyena laughs through broken teeth. "Your mother? Gray coat, scar across one eye? She came through here years ago. Asked for the old road north. Nobody asks for the old road."

**Clue after fight 6**

In the ruins, Wolf finds a howl carved into stone, a pack mark only his mother ever used. Beside it are ten other marks he does not recognize, all pointing the same direction: up.

**Clue after fight 9**

Wolf can smell her now, faint and old, on the wind coming from the north. It is mixed with something else. Something big. Something that should not smell like eleven different animals at once.

**Ending**

The crowd chants his name, but Wolf only hears the silence after it.

He won the crown his mother never came back with. He still does not know why he wanted it, only that she wanted it first.

## Hyena

**Intro**

Everyone thinks Hyena is laughing. Hyena is never laughing.

The sound comes out when he is afraid, and he has been afraid since the night his mother walked into the dark and the whole clan pretended she never existed.

He signed up for the tournament as a joke. That is what he tells himself.

**Clue after fight 3**

The clan elders refused to say her name, but the Leopard he just beat did not.

"Your mother made a deal with the others. Eleven of them, the strongest of their kind. They swore to climb something together."

**Clue after fight 6**

Hyena finds his mother's bone charm hanging from a dead branch at the edge of the old jungle. It was not lost. It was left, like a marker on a trail.

**Clue after fight 9**

Every hyena knows to stay away from the black stone in the north. Hyena always thought it was a story to scare pups.

The charm is warm in his paw. It is pointing at the black stone.

**Ending**

Hyena wins the crown, and for the first time in years, he does not laugh.

He looks north, and something up there looks back.

## Leopard

**Intro**

Leopard is the fastest thing in the jungle, and she has spent her whole life running toward something she cannot see.

Her mother vanished without a trace. Leopards leave no trace. That was the lesson. But a mother should leave something.

Now the tournament has started, and her feet will not let her run anywhere else.

**Clue after fight 3**

The Jaguar circles her after the fight. "Your mother and mine left together. Same night, same moon. Did yours ever tell you where?"

Leopard did not even know they had known each other.

**Clue after fight 6**

Clawed into the bark of the tallest tree, at a height only a leopard could reach: her mother's mark, and an arrow. The arrow does not point across the jungle. It points at the sky.

**Clue after fight 9**

From the treetops, on a clear night, Leopard sees it for the first time: a black tower on the horizon, half hidden in cloud. She has climbed that tree a thousand times. The tower was never there before.

**Ending**

The crown is hers. Leopard stands still for the first time in her life.

It does not feel like an ending. It feels like the starting line.

## Jaguar

**Intro**

Jaguar does not believe in signs. She believes in strength.

But she has dreamed of a crown every night since her mother disappeared, and the dream always ends the same way: her mother climbing, and never reaching the top.

Jaguar has decided to finish the climb herself.

**Clue after fight 3**

Her mother's hunting grounds are untouched, as if she left in the middle of a hunt and never came back for the kill.

In the mud, Jaguar finds tracks. Eleven sets, all different, all heading north together.

**Clue after fight 6**

The Crocodile does not want to talk, but Jaguar makes him. "They called themselves the Pact. Eleven champions, one from every kind. They went to take the crown from whoever wears the real one."

Jaguar has never heard of a real one.

**Clue after fight 9**

Jaguar finds an old trophy wall in a ruined temple: carvings of beasts with their heads bowed before something enormous. Its face has been scratched out by claws, over and over, as if someone could not stand to look at it.

**Ending**

Jaguar takes the crown and holds it up to the light. It is beautiful. It is also smaller than the one in the carving.

This is not the real crown. Somewhere, someone is still wearing that one.

## Crocodile

**Intro**

Crocodile is older than most fighters in the tournament and slower than all of them. He does not care.

He has waited at the edge of the same river for years, waiting for his father to swim back. He is done waiting.

He does not know why the crown matters. He only knows that his father went looking for it.

**Clue after fight 3**

The river talks to those who listen long enough. Tonight it says the same thing three times: eleven crossed here, going north, and none crossed back.

**Clue after fight 6**

In the mud of the riverbed, Crocodile finds one of his father's scales, as big as a shield and scored with claw marks far too large to belong to anything he has ever seen.

**Clue after fight 9**

The old ones of the swamp will not say what lives in the north. They only say this: "Your father was the toughest of us. He bit it once. That is the only reason we still talk about him."

**Ending**

The crown sits on Crocodile's head, and the river is quiet for the first time in years.

Then, far upstream, something heavy slides into the water.

## Lion

**Intro**

Everyone already calls Lion a king. He has never felt like one.

His father ruled before him, until the night he walked out of the pride lands wearing his crown and never came back. The pride says he was a coward who ran.

Lion entered the tournament to prove the pride wrong. He tells himself that is the only reason.

**Clue after fight 3**

An old lioness pulls Lion aside after the fight. "Your father did not run, cub. He went to challenge something. Ten others went with him. They wanted to end it."

"End what?" Lion asks. She does not answer.

**Clue after fight 6**

Lion finds his father's crown half buried at the foot of a cliff, bent and scratched. Kings do not drop their crowns.

Someone took it off him. Someone stronger.

**Clue after fight 9**

Every lion in the pride has the same dream, the old lioness admits. A roar from the north that sounds almost like theirs, but wrong, mixed with a dozen other voices.

Lion heard it last night for the first time. It sounded like his father.

**Ending**

Lion wears the crown his father lost, and the pride finally bows.

But Lion is not looking at them. He is looking north, where the roar came from, and he is starting to understand why his father had to go.

## Tiger

**Intro**

Tiger does not fight to win. Tiger fights because when he stops moving, he remembers.

He remembers the stripes on his mother's back disappearing into the tall grass. He remembers being told she would come back. She did not.

The tournament is just somewhere to keep moving. At least, that is what he thought.

**Clue after fight 3**

The Grizzly he defeated mutters as he falls: "Stripes like those. I have seen them before. Not on a tiger."

Tiger asks what that means. The Grizzly does not get up to explain.

**Clue after fight 6**

In an abandoned camp, Tiger finds a circle of eleven sleeping spots, one shaped exactly like his mother. In the center are ashes, and a single word clawed into a stone: TOGETHER.

**Clue after fight 9**

Hunters in the north have a story about a beast with stripes on its arms and spots on its legs, and a tail that does not belong to a cat.

Tiger tells himself it is just a story. His paws are shaking.

**Ending**

Tiger takes the crown, and for one moment he is perfectly still.

Then he hears it, far away in the north: something moving that carries his mother's stripes.

## Grizzly

**Intro**

Grizzly is a mountain with fur on it. Nobody in the jungle can move him, and nobody has tried in years.

But every spring he wakes from his long sleep expecting to see his father at the mouth of the cave. Every spring the cave is empty.

This year Grizzly woke up early, angry, and pointed north.

**Clue after fight 3**

His father's claw marks are on the trees along the old road, very high and very deep. Grizzly reaches up and puts his own paw beside them. He is bigger now than his father ever was.

It does not make him feel any better.

**Clue after fight 6**

A traveling Hippo tells him: "Your father was the strongest of the eleven. They put him in front, to take the first hit."

"The first hit from what?" Grizzly asks. The Hippo goes quiet.

**Clue after fight 9**

At the top of a ridge, Grizzly finds the place where the road ends. Beyond it, the clouds have a shape, the outline of something tall and black that the wind will not move.

He smells his father there, strongest of all.

**Ending**

Grizzly takes the crown and roars until the mountains answer.

Something else answers too. It is wearing his father's arms.

## Hippo

**Intro**

Hippo is the most dangerous animal in the jungle, and also the most patient. She has waited a long time for her mother to come home.

The herd says she drowned. Hippos do not drown.

When the tournament was announced, Hippo walked out of the water for the first time in years.

**Clue after fight 3**

The Elephant she beats is an old friend of the family. He looks at her sadly. "Your mother made me promise not to tell you. She went with the others to the black tower. All eleven. I should have gone too."

**Clue after fight 6**

In the dried mud where her mother used to rest, Hippo finds tracks leading out of the water. Huge ones, heavier than any hippo. They walk in, circle the spot, and walk back out, as if something came looking for her.

**Clue after fight 9**

The water has been warm all season, the herd says, as if something enormous is breathing somewhere upstream.

Hippo dives down and listens. Far away, under the riverbed, something huge has a heartbeat.

**Ending**

Hippo stands on the top of the Tower, dripping and furious, holding the crown.

She will not wait any longer. She has a tower to climb, even if no one else can see it yet.

## Elephant

**Intro**

Elephant never forgets. That is the problem. He remembers everything except the one thing that matters: where his father went.

He remembers the night his father touched his trunk to his forehead and said "I will be back before the rains." The rains have come eleven times since then.

The crown means nothing to Elephant. Remembering means everything.

**Clue after fight 3**

In the elephant graveyard, every elder has a resting place. Every one except his father's.

There is no body, no bones and no tusks. Elephant searches until sunrise and finds nothing.

**Clue after fight 6**

The Hippo he defeats finally tells him: "Your father led them. He was the one who found the tower. He said it had been waiting for someone to come."

**Clue after fight 9**

Elephant dreams of tusks: his father's tusks, white and curved and unmistakable. In the dream they are not on his father. They are on something else, something with a lion's mane and wings.

Elephant wakes up screaming, and for the first time he wishes he could forget.

**Ending**

Elephant has the crown and the cheers of the whole jungle, and he gives both away.

He is the last one. All eleven have climbed now. All eleven have the same question. Somewhere, a door that was never there is opening.

## The Forgotten Tower (secret)

**Entering**

There was no tower here yesterday.

There is no name above the door, no path leading to it and no sound inside. But every fighter who has ever felt the drums knows this is where they were going.

The door is already open. It has been waiting.

**Echo 1**

The air goes cold. Something steps out of the dark, shaped like a fighter but gray and hollow, with eyes that glow faintly purple.

It moves like someone you almost remember.

**Echo 2**

Another echo blocks the stairs. It fights with a style that is old and proud, the way champions fought a generation ago.

As it falls, it whispers one word: "Climb."

**Echo 3**

The last echo is the strongest, as if it held on the longest. It does not want to stop you. It wants to see if you are strong enough.

When it fades, the stairway up finally lights.

**The summit**

The stairs end at the top of the world. Wind howls through broken pillars. Eleven banners hang here, torn and faded, one for every kind of beast.

You recognize every one of them. They belonged to the ones who climbed before.

**The truth**

Something huge rises from the shadow of the throne.

It has a lion's mane and a crown. It has an elephant's tusks, a tiger's stripes and a leopard's spots. A crocodile's tail drags behind it. A wolf's head hangs at its belt. On its back are wings, and around its shoulders coil serpents.

"I am MAJIC," it says, with a voice made of eleven voices. "Your parents came here as the Pact. Eleven champions, the best of their kind, sworn to take my crown."

"They fought well. They lost. And I keep what I win."

It spreads its arms, and every trophy on its body is someone you have been searching for.

"You felt the drums, little one. That was me, calling the next generation home."

**True ending**

MAJIC falls, and eleven voices leave his body at once, rising like smoke into the dawn.

The parents are not coming back, but they are finally free. And the jungle, for the first time in a generation, has a true king.

**History of the tower**

Long before the tournament, the jungle had no king. It had MAJIC.

He was once a single beast who learned that every challenger he defeated left something behind: strength, speed, armor, a bite. He kept all of it. Over the centuries he grew into something no longer one animal, but all of them.

He built the tower from the black stone of the old mountain and hid it from every map. He created the tournament so the strongest of each generation would rise, climb, and come to him.

One generation ago, eleven champions saw through the lie. They formed the Pact and climbed together. They nearly won. MAJIC took their trophies and called the drums again for their children.

This time, the children came. And this time, the Forgotten Tower lost.
