# KING OF THE JUNGLE: A LEGACY UNLEASHED
## MASTER CHARACTER SPECIAL MOVES & REALISTIC ELEMENTAL EFFECTS PROMPT

### PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Develop a complete cinematic special-move system for every fighter in KING OF THE JUNGLE: A LEGACY UNLEASHED.

Each character must have distinctive special attacks, environmental effects, unique fighting animations, and powerful finishing moves inspired by their animal characteristics.

The game uses high-resolution cinematic 2.5D fighters with realistic animal anatomy, detailed armor, smooth movement, and dramatic combat effects.

All moves must look powerful, natural, and visually impressive while maintaining smooth gameplay on desktop and mobile devices.

Elemental effects are fantasy abilities presented through realistic-looking water, snow, dust, fire, vegetation, electricity, wind, and particle simulations.

## 1. LION: THE ROYAL KING

**Primary Effects:** Golden energy, dust, shockwaves, and roaring wind.

**Special Moves:**

1. **Royal Roar:** Lion releases a thunderous roar, creating a visible golden shockwave that pushes his opponent backward.
2. **King's Claw:** A devastating claw combination leaves brief golden slash trails through the air.
3. **Savannah Quake:** Lion slams both fists into the ground, sending realistic dust, stones, and ground shockwaves toward his opponent.
4. **Predator's Pounce:** Lion leaps forward with a powerful claw strike, creating dust upon landing.
5. **Golden Dominion:** Lion temporarily surrounds himself with a subtle golden aura during a cinematic power attack.

**Ultimate Finisher: KING'S WRATH**

Lion roars, launches his opponent with an uppercut, jumps after them, and delivers a devastating aerial claw strike. The opponent crashes into the ground, creating a massive dust explosion and golden shockwave.

**ULTIMATE FINISHER: THE KINGDOM'S END**

## 16. REALISTIC VISUAL EFFECTS ENGINE

Create a reusable special-effects system supporting:

- Water splashes, ripples, droplets, and reflections
- Snowflakes, frost, ice crystals, and cold mist
- Grass, leaves, roots, and vines
- Dust clouds, flying rocks, and ground cracks
- Wind vortices and air-pressure distortion
- Sparks and lightning-like energy
- Green venom mist and particles
- Fire-like energy trails
- Character-specific glowing slash effects
- Cinematic shockwaves
- Dynamic impact flashes
- Temporary arena lighting changes

Effects must appear connected to the fighter's actual movements.

Water must splash at the point of impact.

Snowflakes must swirl naturally around Snow Leopard.

Leaves and grass must react to Stag's movements.

Dust must rise when heavy characters slam the ground.

Feathers must move naturally with Eagle's wings.

Avoid flat cartoon effects, oversized opaque particles, and visual clutter that obscures gameplay.

## 17. SPECIAL MOVE CONTROLS

Integrate special moves into the existing fighting system.

Support keyboard, controller, and mobile touch controls.

Use configurable directional inputs and attack-button combinations.

Example control scheme:

- Down, Forward + Light Attack: Special Move 1
- Down, Back + Heavy Attack: Special Move 2
- Forward, Down, Forward + Heavy Attack: Special Move 3
- Back, Forward + Special Button: Signature Move
- Full Special Meter + Special Button: Ultimate Finisher

Make inputs adjustable to the existing game controls.

For mobile, provide an optional dedicated Special button that does not interfere with movement, blocking, jumping, or normal attacks.

Include move lists accessible from the pause menu and character selection.

## 18. ANIMATION REQUIREMENTS

Each fighter must receive:

- Five distinct special moves
- One ultimate cinematic finisher
- Unique elemental visual effects
- Appropriate sound effects
- Character-specific attack animations
- Impact and recovery animations
- Smooth transitions back to fighting stance

MAJIC receives ten special moves and one ultimate finisher.

Maintain the approved cinematic 2.5D character appearances.

All special moves must use continuous fluid motion with no frozen limbs, pose snapping, teleporting body parts, or disappearing accessories.

Every limb must move naturally according to the action.

Use motion-capture-style animation principles, realistic body weight, anticipation, follow-through, and recovery.

Do not stretch a single static character image to simulate an entire attack.

Where new animation frames are required, create matching character-specific animations.

## 19. GAMEPLAY BALANCE

Every special move must have appropriate startup, active, and recovery frames.

Assign configurable damage, range, cooldown, meter cost, and hit reactions.

Powerful moves must have meaningful weaknesses or resource requirements.

Allow blocking, counterattacks, combo breakers, and appropriate defensive responses.

MAJIC may have enhanced abilities as the final tower boss, but his unlockable playable version must follow competitive balance rules.

Keep cosmetic clothing and paid items separate from combat statistics.

## 20. MOBILE & DESKTOP PERFORMANCE

Optimize all effects for the existing web-based game.

Use sprite-based particles, reusable texture atlases, object pooling, and efficient rendering where appropriate.

Provide graphics settings:

- Low: Reduced particle count and simplified effects
- Medium: Balanced effects
- High: Full cinematic particles and enhanced lighting

Maintain readable combat action on smaller mobile screens.

Preserve landscape gameplay support.

Avoid excessive screen shake, flashing, and particle density.

Provide options to reduce screen shake and flashing effects.
